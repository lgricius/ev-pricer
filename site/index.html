Data comes from the public report of the Lithuanian charging-point information system ev.vialietuva.lt, as submitted by the operators, and is refreshed hourly. Prices may lag behind the operators' current tariffs, so always check the price in the charging network's app before you plug in. Prices shown are the basic pay-as-you-go rates and do not include subscription plans, saving plans or loyalty discounts. "Free*" means the operator reported "Nemokama" and is often limited to a venue's customers.
